Browser diagnostic

This page checks what your browser can do. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. Work down the page, then copy the Results box at the end and send it back.

1. Automatic report

This box fills in by itself. You do not need to do anything here.

2. Live region test

Activate the button below. Your screen reader should then speak or braille the words "live region test succeeded" without you moving to it. It may take up to two seconds.

Did your screen reader announce anything by itself after you pressed that button? Type yes or no, and what it said, in the notes box in section 5.

3. Toggle button test

The button below is a toggle. Move to it and note how your screen reader describes it, then activate it and note what changes. We expect something like "not pressed" or "off" first, then "pressed" or "on".

4. Microphone test

Please read before activating. The button below asks for permission to use the microphone. A permission prompt may appear that you have to answer. Nothing is recorded and no audio leaves the device.
